Squid Run — Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026

Squid Run collects no personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Your location

The app uses your iPhone's location to show the map of your area, suggest loops that start where you are and record your run, including with the screen off. Squid Run does not collect your location: routes are calculated on your iPhone and are not sent to anyone. To name streets (the loop's name, the starting point), the app queries Apple's map service, which then receives the location concerned.

Your runs

The history of your runs is stored on your iPhone only.

Downloading maps

To work without a connection, the app downloads the streets of your area from a storage server (OVHcloud, in France). As with any web page, this server sees the IP address of the device doing the download, but receives neither your location nor any information about you. Squid Run does not keep these logs.

Apple services

The base map, address search and street names are provided by Apple Maps, and the Premium subscription is sold and managed by the App Store. These services are covered by Apple's privacy policy. Squid Run receives no payment information.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app erases everything it has stored on your iPhone.
