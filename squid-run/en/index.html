Squid Run

Running loops, by day and by night

Squid Run suggests running loops that start from where you are, at the distance you choose, avoiding dangerous roads and, at night, keeping to lit streets. While you run, the app guides you turn by turn, even on the Lock Screen.

Support

A question, an odd loop, a street described incorrectly? Write to baptiste.decobert@mailo.com.

A mistake on a street (pavement, lighting, footpath) can also be corrected directly on OpenStreetMap: it will be fixed for everyone at the next map update.

Premium

The loop is free. The Premium subscription adds four route shapes: the figure of eight, the lollipop, the out-and-back and the neighbourhood exploration. You can cancel at any time in your App Store account settings.
